# ECOSYSTEM.md

**The rolldabones governance ecosystem · v2.1.6 · 30 October 2026 (Korea Standard Time, KST)**

**This is the single canonical map. Every other repository links here from a short "Part of the ecosystem" section rather than duplicating this content. One map, many pointers: duplicated maps drift.**

Everything in this account serves one practice: enterprise AI governance, risk management and compliance that is evidence-led and auditable. Three doctrines run through all of it, stated here in the sentence forms of [DOCTRINE.md](DOCTRINE.md) section 8. **Slow AI**: AI that cannot be governed, explained and audited is not deployed; the three tests are conjunctive, and the doctrine is a precondition, not a speed limit. **Informed Intent**: no AI system acts without prior authorization stating its purpose, authority, boundaries and exit, given by a human who meets the five authorizer conditions. **Final Liability rests with the Human**: every material outcome attaches to a named human owner with decision rights, oversight and the power to intervene. The **GRCnext™** framework supplies the operating primitives: Services, Tolerances, Pipes, Switches, Exits.

The paragraph above is the summary. The normative statement of the three doctrines lives in [DOCTRINE.md](DOCTRINE.md), beside this file: one definition each, the allocation lifecycle, the drafting rule reserving liability language for where liability genuinely lands, the statutory analogues and the sanctioned short forms (section 8). Repositories cite DOCTRINE.md rather than restating the doctrines; a repository that compresses a doctrine copies a short form verbatim. The retirement of the account's seven custom GPTs on 1 October 2026 is recorded once in [RETIREMENT.md](RETIREMENT.md), also beside this file.

## How the layers connect

Doctrine (Layer 1) defines what governed means, the assessment tools (Layer 2) operationalize it at the decision point, the guidance repositories (Layer 3) carry it to boards, Clients and practitioners and the field studies (Layer 4) test it against live systems. One sentence per repository, in layer order:

1. Layer 1, grc states what governed means on the OCEG spine: the operating cycle and the elements every instrument downstream assumes.
2. grc-workbook builds that capability module by module and carries the Governance Test, whose demand for owners, evidence and systems of record the artifact factory answers.
3. slow-ai-kitchen operates the specification day to day: twelve steps with gates, roles and tiers through which governed work runs.
4. definition-of-done closes the loop: no tool's output is accepted until a named human confirms it against tests defined before the work began, the depth work inside the Kitchen's Gates 1, 6 and 7.
5. origami-method turns the definition into workflow-design discipline: its creases are Tolerances on inputs and outputs, its gates are Switches and its fold log is controlled change under audit.
6. final-liability-rests-with-the-human-book-wip argues the Final Liability doctrine at length and supplies the AI Audit and Due Diligence Checklist and the AI GRC Master Reference against which an audit tests the chain.
7. computational-drafting supplies the specification layer beneath the whole chain: the eight-element artifact that Informed Intent authorizes, the Origami Method folds into workflow, the Kitchen operates, the assessment tools interrogate and the Definition of Done accepts.
8. Layer 2, AI-Impact-Assessment-Tool operationalizes the doctrine at the deployment decision: its kill-switch requirement is a GRCnext™ Switch, its decommission plan an Exit, its data flow narrative a Pipe, its thresholds Tolerances and its system inventory a Service.
9. risk-informed-decision-making-prompt carries the same discipline into continuous decisions under uncertainty, leaving an auditable work product at each one.
10. RedCap-00 tests whether the Pipes, Switches and Exits would actually execute under stress, within 72 hours.
11. RedCap-01 makes decision boundaries into usable Tolerances and trigger-to-action links into Switches, testing whether ERM improves the decisions that determine objective achievement.
12. master-prompt-for-in-house-legal-and-compliance runs the in-house legal and compliance work the chain generates under the same evidence discipline, holding substantive work until a human has stated the matter.
13. AI-GRC-Copilot turns the doctrine into paperwork that proves itself, converting the organization's facts into the 30 artifacts whose owners, evidence and systems of record the Governance Test demands.
14. AI-GRC-Master-List-of-Questions captures those facts once, in 17 sections, so the Copilot drafts from one answered input set.
15. GRCnext-Copilot is the primitives run as an engine, scoring whether Pipes, Switches and Exits would actually execute within tolerance.
16. Contract-Mechanism-Review-Assistant reads the agreements those Exits depend on, as risk-transfer mechanisms with evidence anchors and an uncertainty register.
17. Layer 3, ai-governance-for-boards carries the doctrines to the board as mandate, operating discipline and jurisdiction guides and receives the "verify with counsel" flags the assessment tools raise.
18. AI-Governance-Academy carries them to Clients as the engagement templates on the offerings ladder and the publications that argue the case.
19. the-ai-generalist is the widest on-ramp, teaching the minimum control set to working professionals in a day.
20. Layer 4, the-ungoverned-channel tests the doctrines against live systems and feeds corrections back into the tools.

## Maintenance protocol

1. This file is the only canonical map. Repositories link to it; they do not copy it.
2. When a repository is added, renamed or retired, update this file in the same commit series and log the change below.
3. Each repository carries the standard "Part of the ecosystem" section (see the snippet in any current repository README) listing this map plus up to five nearest neighbors.
4. Repositories not yet listed here are pending classification. Add them to a layer on their next substantive revision.
5. **Lockstep in the profile repository.** This file's masthead version, the profile README's masthead version, CITATION.cff and the repository tag are one number and move together: a release that ships a change to this file or to the README bumps all four. DOCTRINE.md keeps its own version series, because it versions the canon and not the repository. Rewritten 17 October 2026: the two series this item once kept apart have coincided since v1.7.10, so they are kept as one.
6. **Doctrine delta rule.** Slow AI, Informed Intent and Final Liability are used across the suite exactly as stated in [DOCTRINE.md](DOCTRINE.md). Repositories add instruments, not doctrine. Restating a doctrine at a different altitude, board, enterprise or task, is permitted; varying the rule is not. Promoted to this protocol from definition-of-done README design principle 5, which stated it for that repository alone.
7. **Versioning exception, logged.** Lockstep versioning across all files in a repository remains the account convention. computational-drafting is a sanctioned exception: its computational-drafting header rule is confined to that repository by decision of 9 August 2026 and does not generalize. master-prompt-for-in-house-legal-and-compliance is a second sanctioned exception, logged 20 October 2026: the master prompt and its test suite keep their own version series (v2.1), separate from the repository version (v2.5.3 as at that date). No other exception is in force.
8. LICENSE.md is the filename convention for new repositories. Five repositories created in the 14 July 2026 cohort use LICENSE, which was a logged decision at the time and is left alone. Both filenames are valid to GitHub; neither is renamed retroactively.
9. **Reciprocity.** A neighbor pointer is returned: a repository named as a nearest neighbor in another repository's "Part of the ecosystem" section names that repository back, or this file records why not on a line naming both repositories and the words "not returned". The usual reason is the five-neighbor cap of item 3. A one-way pointer that is neither returned nor recorded on that list breaks this item. Added 17 October 2026.
