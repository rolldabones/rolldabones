## A Client's own use

CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 defines NonCommercial as "not primarily intended for or directed towards commercial advantage or monetary compensation" (Section 1(k)). An organization that uses an instrument here in its own operations, a business running its AI governance on the Slow AI Kitchen or a legal department running the master prompt on live matters, may fall outside that definition. Whether a given use does is a question of fact the license does not settle. If your use may fall outside it, ask before you rely on the material.

## The two licenses do not mix

CC BY-SA 4.0 and CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 are not compatible. Creative Commons lists no NonCommercial license as compatible with BY-SA 4.0, and no license outside the BY-NC-SA family as compatible with BY-NC-SA 4.0 ([creativecommons.org/compatiblelicenses](https://creativecommons.org/compatiblelicenses), read 9 November 2026). A licensee who adapts material from grc, grc-workbook or the-ungoverned-channel shares the adaptation under BY-SA and cannot fold it into an adaptation of a NonCommercial repository shared under BY-NC-SA.

The Consultant Workbook in [slow-ai-kitchen](https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen/blob/main/consultant-workbook.md) (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0) draws on grc-workbook (CC BY-SA 4.0) as the author's own work: the author holds the copyright in the grc-workbook text it adapts and, as licensor, may offer his adaptation under another license. It carries none of the OCEG GRC Capability Model's named components, elements or outcomes. A licensee adapting grc-workbook does not have that freedom.
